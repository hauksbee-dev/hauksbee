# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to get something right.

hauksbee is a tool people use to decide whether to spend money fabricating a
board, so most disagreements here are technical and settle with evidence: a
board file, a measurement, a datasheet page. Argue about the work, not the
person doing it.

## What is expected

- Take other people's problems seriously, including the ones you find obvious.
  A confusing error message is a real bug report.
- Say what you actually measured. "This check fires on my board and I think it
  is wrong because the datasheet says X" is useful; "this is broken" is not.
- Accept being wrong without fuss, and let other people do the same. Several
  things in this codebase exist because someone's first answer was wrong.
- Respect a maintainer's no. This is a small project and scope is finite.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments about someone or about the
groups they belong to, sustained disruption, publishing anyone's private
information, or unwelcome sexual attention. None of it is welcome here, in
issues, pull requests, commit messages, or anywhere the project is discussed.

## Reporting

Raise it privately with the maintainers through GitHub. Reports are handled
discreetly and in confidence; you will not be asked to argue your case in
public. Anyone who breaks the above may have their contributions removed and
their participation ended, without a debate about degrees.

Security issues follow a different route: see `SECURITY.md`.

## Scope

This applies in the project's own spaces and wherever someone is representing
the project.
